Care-management targeting prototype

Predict next-year admission, group members into phenotypes, then choose whom to contact with limited capacity. Research prototype on CMS synthetic Medicare claims (DE-SynPUF Sample 1).

Synthetic data, assumed effects. CMS DE-SynPUF is synthetic. No clinical or epidemiological conclusion is valid from these numbers. The intervention effect (10–30% fewer admissions) is an assumption, not a measurement. Only a randomized pilot can measure it.
